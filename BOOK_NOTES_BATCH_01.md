## 7) 王亭之《中州派紫微斗数深造讲义》

- Coverage: `Medium` (structure-level + doctrine spine)
- Confidence: `Medium-High`

### Structure learned
- Upper focus: `星曜论` (star nature logic)
- Lower focus: `宫垣论` (12-palace interpretation system)
- Core thematic architecture:
  1. `十四正曜`
  2. supporting/auxiliary + adverse stars
  3. dynamic conditions (`庙陷`, combinations, interactions)
  4. palace-level meaning and relational reading

### Core lessons (English translation)
- `三方四正` is not optional: read each palace with its relational triangle and axis.
- Star meaning is conditional, not static:
  - position (`宫位`)
  - dignity/condition (`庙旺陷`)
  - transformation and combinations
- Methodological posture: anti-superstition rhetoric and emphasis on verifiable patterning appears in the modern transmission narrative.

## 16) 王弼《周易注》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `High` for macro-framework, `Medium` for school-specific details

### Core philosophical engine
- `言 -> 象 -> 意` (words -> images -> meaning-intent)
- Interpretive rule often summarized as:
  - `得象忘言` (grasp image, move beyond wording fixation)
  - `得意忘象` (grasp intent, move beyond image fixation)

### English lesson
- Do not mistake the textual token for final meaning.
- Words and images are vehicles to reach principle-level understanding.
- This stance critiques over-literal symbolic accumulation and shaped later `义理` traditions.
