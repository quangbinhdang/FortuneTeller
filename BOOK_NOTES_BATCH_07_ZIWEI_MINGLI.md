### 11A) 论日为主 — The Day-Master-Centric Framework

**Original Chinese (core doctrine)**:
> 「予尝观唐书所载，有李虚中者，取人所生年月日时干支生克，论命之贵贱寿夭之说，已详之矣！至于宋时，方有子平之说；取日干为主，以年为根，以月为苗，以日为花，以时为果；以生旺死绝休囚制化，决人生休咎。其理必然矣，复有何疑哉！」

**English**: "I have examined Tang-dynasty records; Li Xuzhong took the birth year-month-day-hour stem-branch generation-overcoming to discuss destiny's rank, longevity, etc.—already detailed. By the Song era, the Zi-Ping doctrine emerged: take the Day Stem as master; year as root, month as sprout, day as flower, hour as fruit. Using birth/prosper/death/extinction/retire/imprison/control/transform — determine life's fortune and calamity. The principle is inevitable—what doubt remains?"

**The Five Relationship Classes (original)**:
> 「一曰官，分之阴阳，曰官、曰杀，甲乙见庚辛也；二曰财，分之阴阳，曰正财、偏财，甲乙见戊己也；三曰生气之阴阳，曰印绶、曰倒食，甲乙见壬癸是也；四曰窃气之阴阳，曰食神、曰伤官，甲乙见丙丁是也；五曰同类之阴阳，曰劫财、曰阳刃，甲乙见甲乙是也。大抵贵贱寿夭死生，皆不出于五者；倘有妄立格局，从列其名，而无实用，其飞天禄马、倒冲井栏叉，即伤官，析而为之。举此一端，馀可知矣！」

**English**: "1. Officer [guan], divided yin-yang: Officer and Killing — Jia/Yi meeting Geng/Xin. 2. Wealth [cai], divided: Straight Wealth and Slanted Wealth — Jia/Yi meeting Wu/Ji. 3. Life-generating qi, divided: Seal/Grant and Inverted-Eating — Jia/Yi meeting Ren/Gui. 4. Qi-stealing, divided: Eating-Spirit and Hurting-Officer — Jia/Yi meeting Bing/Ding. 5. Same-kind, divided: Robber-Wealth and Yang-Ren — Jia/Yi meeting Jia/Yi. Generally, rank, longevity, life and death — none exceeds these five. As for those who recklessly establish patterns, listing names without practical use — Flying-Horse, Inverted-Rush, Well-Railing — these are just Hurting-Officer derivatives, divided in name only. From this one example, the rest can be known!"

**Key anti-mechanical doctrine (original)**:
> 「以日为主，大要看日加临于甚度，或身旺？或身弱？又看地支有何格局？金木水火土之数；后看月令中金木水火土，何者旺？又看岁运有何旺？却次日下消详。此非是拘之一隅之说也。」
> 「论命者切不可泥之，月令消详；故表而出之。」

**English**: "Taking the Day as master — greatly observe what degree the Day descends upon: is the self prosperous or weak? Then examine what patterns the Earth Branches contain — the number of Metal/Wood/Water/Fire/Earth. Then examine within the Month-Command: which of the five phases is prosperous? Then examine what is prosperous in the year-fortune cycle. Then dissolve and detail beneath the Day. This is not a doctrine confined to one corner."
"Do not rigidly adhere; dissolve and detail the Month-Command — hence it is set forth here explicitly."

### 11B) 干支暗藏 — Hidden Stems and Branches Methodology

**Original Chinese (地支藏遁歌 — the canonical hiding-song)**:
> 「子宫癸水在其中，丑癸辛金己土同；寅宫甲木兼丙戊，卯宫乙木独相逢。辰藏乙戊三分癸，巳中庚金丙戊丛；午宫丁火并己土，未宫乙己丁共宗。申位庚金壬水戊，酉宫辛金独丰隆；戌宫辛金及丁戊，亥藏壬甲是真踪。」

**English translation**:

| Branch | Hidden Stems | English |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 子 (Zi) | 癸 (Gui) | Gui Water alone within |
| 丑 (Chou) | 癸辛己 (Gui, Xin, Ji) | Gui Water, Xin Metal, Ji Earth together |
| 寅 (Yin) | 甲丙戊 (Jia, Bing, Wu) | Jia Wood + Bing Fire + Wu Earth |
| 卯 (Mao) | 乙 (Yi) | Yi Wood alone |
| 辰 (Chen) | 乙戊癸 (Yi, Wu, Gui) | Yi Wood, Wu Earth, Gui Water (three portions) |
| 巳 (Si) | 庚丙戊 (Geng, Bing, Wu) | Geng Metal, Bing Fire, Wu Earth cluster |
| 午 (Wu) | 丁己 (Ding, Ji) | Ding Fire + Ji Earth |
| 未 (Wei) | 乙己丁 (Yi, Ji, Ding) | Yi Wood, Ji Earth, Ding Fire together |
| 申 (Shen) | 庚壬戊 (Geng, Ren, Wu) | Geng Metal, Ren Water, Wu Earth |
| 酉 (You) | 辛 (Xin) | Xin Metal alone, abundant |
| 戌 (Xu) | 辛丁戊 (Xin, Ding, Wu) | Xin Metal + Ding Fire + Wu Earth |
| 亥 (Hai) | 壬甲 (Ren, Jia) | Ren Water + Jia Wood — true trace |

**节气管藏 seasonal rule (original)**:
> 「立春念三丙火用，馀日甲木旺提纲。」...「大雪七日壬水管；冬至癸水更潺汪。小寒七日癸水养，八日辛金丑库藏；大寒十日己土胜，术者精研仔细详。」

**English gloss**: Each solar term divides the month's qi into precise day-counts of dominant hidden stems. For example, 立春 (Start of Spring): Bing Fire governs for the first 3 days; the remaining days are dominated by Jia Wood. This encodes a micro-seasonal qi allocation within each month.

### 11C) 格局 System — Pattern Classification

The text provides a systematic hierarchy of 十神 relationships, each with dedicated theory chapters:

#### 正官 (Straight Officer) — core chapter
> 「正官乃贵气之物，大忌刑冲破害。...既用提纲作正官，年时支干位，或有一偏官便杂矣！」
> "Straight Officer is noble-qi substance — greatly taboo clash/break/harm/punish. ... Using Month-Command as Straight Officer, if year/hour branch-stem positions contain a Slanted Officer, it is mixed!"

Key rule: 官星要纯不要杂 (Officer star must be pure, not mixed). 喜身旺印绶 (likes self-prosperous and Seal/Grant). 忌伤官 (taboo Hurting-Officer).

#### 偏官/七杀 (Slanted Officer / Seven Killings)
> 「偏官即七杀，要制伏，盖偏官七杀即小人...御得其道则驯伏。」
> "Slanted Officer = Seven Killings — must be controlled and subdued. Seven Killings is the petty person... if controlled properly, it becomes tamed and useful."

Core rule: 有制伏则为偏官，无制伏则为七杀 — with control it's Slanted Officer; without control it's Seven Killings.
> 「杀无刃不显，刃无杀不威」— "Killing without Ren has no display; Ren without Killing has no authority."

#### 正财/偏财 (Straight / Slanted Wealth)
> 「财要得时，不要财多。若财多则自家日本有力，可以胜任，当化作官。」
> "Wealth must obtain its season; do not desire much wealth. If wealth is much, one's own Day-root must have strength to bear it; then it transforms into Office."

#### 印绶 (Seal/Grant)
> 「有官无印，即非真官；有印无官，反成其福。」
> "Having Officer without Seal — not true Officer. Having Seal without Officer — inversely becomes fortune."

#### 食神 (Eating-Spirit)
> 「食神有气胜财官，先要他强旺本干。」
> "Eating-Spirit having qi surpasses Wealth and Officer — first requires it to strengthen and prosper the root-stem."

#### 伤官 (Hurting-Officer)
> 「伤官伤尽最为奇，尤恐伤多返不宜。」
> "Hurting-Officer hurt to completion is most strange — yet fear: when hurting is excessive, it recoils inadequately."

### 11E) 论疾病 (On Illness) — Chapter-Level Extraction

**Original Chinese (organ-stem correspondence)**:
> 「甲肝，乙胆，丙小肠，丁心，戊胃，己脾乡，庚是大肠，辛属肺，壬是膀胱，癸肾脏。」

**English**: "Jia = liver, Yi = gallbladder, Bing = small intestine, Ding = heart, Wu = stomach, Ji = spleen, Geng = large intestine, Xin = lungs, Ren = bladder, Gui = kidneys."

**Outer-body correspondence**:
> 「甲头，乙项，丙肩求，丁心，戊胁，己属腹，庚系人脐，辛为股，壬胫，癸足自来求。」

**English**: "Jia = head, Yi = neck, Bing = shoulders, Ding = heart-area, Wu = ribs, Ji = abdomen, Geng = navel, Xin = thighs, Ren = calves, Gui = feet."

**Branch-body correspondence**:
> 「子疝气，丑肚腹，寅臂肢，卯目手，辰背胸，巳面齿，午心腹，未脾胸，申咳疾，酉肝肺，戌背肺，亥头肝。」

**Five-Phase illness methodology (original)**:
> 「八字干支，五行生克之义，取伤重者而断之；五行干支太旺、不及俱病。」

**English**: "The eight-character stems and branches, by the meaning of five-phase generation and overcoming — take the most severely injured and judge from it; five phases, stems, and branches excessively prosperous or insufficiently so — both cause illness."

**Specific phase-illness correlations**:

| Phase Condition | Original | English |

| 水值土伤 | 「下元冷疾，只缘水值土伤」 | "Lower-origin cold illness — only because Water encounters Earth's wounding" |

### 11G) 论大运 (On Great Fortune Cycles)

**Original doctrine**:
> 「子平之法，大运看支，岁君看干，交运同接木。」
> "Zi-Ping method: Great Fortune looks at the branch; Year Sovereign looks at the stem. Exchanging fortune is like grafting wood."

**干旺宜行衰运，干弱宜旺运** (Original):
> "When the stem is prosperous, suitable to travel decline-fortune; when the stem is weak, suitable for prosperous fortune. This is: the weak seeking qi-prosperity's borrowing; excess leading to insufficiency's management. Must comprehend the adaptation."

**转角接木 doctrine**: When fortune changes between 丑-寅, 辰-巳, 未-申, 戌-亥 (corner-turning transitions), these are the most dangerous fortune-exchange points — "格局凶者死，格局善者灾" (pattern-inauspicious: death; pattern-good: calamity).

**English**: "Question: The ten stems have yin-yang, hard-soft, birth-death distinctions — is this saying correct? Answer: Ten stems = five yang + five yin. Yang is hard; yin is soft. ### 10B) 卷二·论十干合 — Five Stem-Combination Types

**English**: "Combination is the meaning of harmony. Yang meeting yang — two yang competing becomes overcoming. Yin meeting yin — two yin insufficient becomes overcoming. This anti-mechanical approach is the Zhongzhou school's defining contribution to Zi Wei methodology.

4. **动态三方四正 (Dynamic San-Fang Si-Zheng)**: The three-direction/four-orthodox scope must be read dynamically — the same stars in triplicity mean different things when the native enters a different decade palace.

#### 四、武曲星 (Wuqu) — The Finance Star

**Original Chinese**:
> 「武曲星在五行属阴金，在天上属北斗星，化为财，是财帛宫的主星。在人命中，能发亦能败。武曲星最忌化忌星，则事业失败。会煞星，则焦头烂额，不堪收拾。最喜化禄，则财源涌到，事业发展，威名远震。」

**English**: "Wuqu Star — Five Phases: Yin Metal. In heaven: belongs to Northern Dipper. Transforms to Wealth. Is the Lord Star of the Wealth Palace. In human destiny: can prosper, can also fail. Wuqu star most taboos transforming to Taboo [hua ji] — then career fails. Meeting baleful stars — then scorched head, burnt forehead, beyond recovery. Most delights in transforming to Prosperity [hua lu] — then wealth-sources surge in, career develops, authority and fame shake far."

**王亭之注 (Wang Tingzhi's key note)**:
> 「武曲不喜见昌曲，因为彼此气质不同。亦不喜见陀罗、铃星，所以有"铃昌陀武，限至投河"的说法。」
> "Wuqu dislikes seeing Wen Chang and Wen Qu, because their qi-substances differ. Also dislikes seeing Tuoluo and Lingxing — hence the saying 'Bell-Wen-Tuo-Wu, limit reaches [= when this combination appears], throw into the river.'"

**武曲天府同宫**:
> 「"武曲天府同宫于子午，主有寿。" ...武曲为财星，天府为财库，所以同宫便基本上主吉利。」

**English**: "Wuqu-Tianfu sharing palace at Zi/Wu — indicates longevity. ... Wuqu is the Wealth Star; Tianfu is the Wealth Treasury — hence sharing a palace fundamentally indicates auspiciousness."

#### 五、天同星 (Tiantong) — The Fortune Star

**Original Chinese**:
> 「天同星在五行属阳水，在天属南斗星群，化作福星，是命盘中福德宫的主星。禀性温和，不畏擎羊星的凶焰，不忧化忌星捣乱。所以天同星在午宫与擎羊星同度，称为"马头带箭格"，反主为国效劳，掌握兵符大权，是立功战场的大将。」

**English**: "Tiantong Star — Five Phases: Yang Water. In heaven: belongs to Southern Dipper star-group. Transforms to Fortune Star. Is the Lord Star of the Fortune-Virtue Palace. Disposition: gentle and warm. Does not fear Qingyang star's ferocious flames; does not worry about Hua-ji star causing disruption. Hence Tiantong at Wu palace sharing degree with Qingyang is called 'Horse-Head Carrying Arrow Pattern' — inversely indicates serving the nation, holding military-tally authority, a great general achieving merit on the battlefield."

**王亭之注 (Wang Tingzhi's note)**:
> 「天同为福星。所以有"居十二宫中皆为福泽"的说法，不过这个说法实在相当片面，因为它的福泽，可能要先经艰危困苦而来。天同为纯粹精神型的星曜，所以在一般情形下，嫌其人过分情绪化，因而影响事业，必须经过艰危，然后始有奋发之心。」

**English**: "Tiantong is the Fortune Star. Hence the saying 'dwelling in any of the twelve palaces is all fortune and grace' — but this saying is actually quite one-sided, because its fortune and grace may first require passing through hardship, danger, and suffering. Tiantong is a purely spiritual-type star — hence in ordinary circumstances, it is faulted for making the person excessively emotional, thereby affecting career. Must pass through hardship, then only begins to have a striving heart."

#### 六、廉贞星 (Lianzhen) — The Prison / Secondary Peach-Blossom Star

**Original Chinese**:
> 「廉贞星在五行属阴火，在天属北斗星群，化为囚。在命盘是次桃花。但廉贞在未宫守命，化禄星或在寅、申宫守命，会禄存，反是清白上格。」

**English**: "Lianzhen Star — Five Phases: Yin Fire. In heaven: belongs to Northern Dipper star-group. Transforms to Prison. In the destiny chart: secondary Peach-Blossom. But Lianzhen at Wei palace guarding the Ming [Life], transforming to Hua-lu, or at Yin/Shen palaces guarding the Ming, meeting Lu-cun — inversely is the pure-innocent top pattern."

**王亭之注 (Wang Tingzhi's note)**:
> 「廉贞为'次桃花'，但此性质却仅主在精神上有异性的感触，不主实际行动。只有当廉贞与贪狼同度，或与天姚、咸池、沐浴等桃花杂曜同会时，然后才转变为真正的桃花。」
> "Lianzhen is 'secondary Peach-Blossom,' but this nature only indicates spiritual-level opposite-sex sensitivity, not actual conduct. Only when Lianzhen shares degree with Tanlang, or assembles with peach-blossom miscellaneous stars like Tianyao, Xianchi, Muyu — then only does it transform to true Peach-Blossom."

### 8D) 星曜飞躔法则 (Star-Palace Reaction Principle) — Original

> 「一个星曜，好似一块塑胶，每一个宫又好似不同的模型，所以星曜飞到某一宫时，其外型便被该宫的模型所改变，可是它的本质并没有改变。」

**English**: "A star-luminary is like a piece of plastic; each palace is like a different mold. So when a star flies to a certain palace, its external form is changed by that palace's mold — but its essence has not changed."

This is the foundational metaphor of the entire interpretative system: stars have invariant essences, but their manifestation varies by the palace they occupy.
