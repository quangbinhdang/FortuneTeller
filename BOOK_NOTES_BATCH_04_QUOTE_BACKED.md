## 11) 《渊海子平》 (quote-backed extraction)

### Evidence quotes
- `以日为主，年为本，月为提纲，时为辅佐。`
- `取日干为主，以年为根，以月为苗，以日为花，以时为果。`
- `大抵贵贱寿夭死生，皆不出于五者。`

### English translation + lesson
- "Take the Day Stem as the master; year as root; month as governing framework; hour as support."
- "Use the Day Master as the center; year as root, month as sprout, day as flower, hour as fruit."
- "Status, longevity, and life outcomes all fall within the five relationship classes."

**Learned doctrine:** this text hard-centers day-master relational logic and gives a full-stack hierarchy of temporal pillars. It warns against fake pattern proliferation detached from functional use.

## 14) 《穷通宝鉴》 (quote-backed extraction)

### Evidence quotes
- `故高者抑之使平，下者举之使崇，或益其不及，或损其太过。`
- `故高者抑之...归于中道，使无有馀不足之累。`
- `三春甲木...是以水火二物，要得时相济为美。`

### English translation + lesson
- "Raise what is too low, restrain what is too high; supplement deficiency and reduce excess."
- "Return to the middle way so there is no burden of excess or insufficiency."
- "For spring Jia Wood, water and fire must be seasonally balanced to be excellent."

**Learned doctrine:** this is a regulation system (`调候`) built around seasonal equilibrium, not simple label assignment.

## 16) 王弼《周易注》 (quote-backed extraction)

### Evidence quotes
- `夫象者，出意者也；言者，明象者也。`
- `得象而忘言；得意而忘象。`
- `忘象以求其意，义斯见矣。`

### English translation + lesson
- "Image comes from meaning-intent; words clarify image."
- "Once image is grasped, release fixation on words; once intent is grasped, release fixation on image."
- "By moving beyond image-fixation to seek intent, meaning appears."

**Learned doctrine:** hermeneutic ascent from text -> image -> principle; anti-literalism without discarding textual method.

## 18) 《周易本义》 (quote-backed extraction)

### Evidence quotes
- `《易》之为书，卦、爻、彖、象之义备，而天地万物之情见。`
- `太极者，道也；两仪者，阴阳也。`
- `《易》本卜筮之书` (core Zhu Xi position in broader tradition)

### English translation + lesson
- "The Yi includes the meanings of hexagrams, lines, judgments, and images, through which the principles of heaven-earth and myriad things are disclosed."
- "Taiji is Dao; the Two Modes are yin and yang."
- "The Yi is fundamentally a divinatory text."

**Learned doctrine:** Zhu Xi integrates cosmological-philosophical reading with disciplined divinatory function.
