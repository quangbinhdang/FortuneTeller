## 10) 万民英《三命通会》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `Medium-High`

### Learned structure
- Traditional 12-volume architecture.
- Integrates cosmology (`太极`, `阴阳`, `五行`) with applied fate-analysis procedures.
- Strong compendium character: collects and systematizes prior streams.

### English synthesis
- The text is a major "system-builder" for post-classical BaZi learning.
- It should be read as both:
  1. doctrinal consolidation, and
  2. practical interpretive manual.
- Caution: later critiques note over-reliance risks if read mechanically.

## 11) 徐升《渊海子平》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `Medium`

### Learned structure
- Canonical early pillar text for `子平` method lines.
- Emphasizes day-master centric logic (`日干为主`) and patterning (`格局`) discourse.

### English synthesis
- Introduces enduring grammar for relation mapping between day-master and other stems/branches.
- Classical maxim spirit: pattern quality depends on dynamic balancing/constraint, not one-label glorification.

## 12) 沈孝瞻《子平真诠》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `High` for doctrine core

### Core doctrine learned
- `八字用神，专求月令`:
  - useful-god selection must anchor in seasonal/month-command context.
- Distinguishes "favorable-use" and "adverse-use" classes, requiring different handling logic.

### English synthesis
- Month-command (`月令`) is the climate engine of chart interpretation.
- Correct use-god selection is the pivot; many later misreadings come from de-centering seasonality.

## 13) 《滴天髓》（任铁樵阐微传统）

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `High` for anti-mechanical stance

### Core doctrine learned
- Rejects mechanical catalogism of exotic patterns and arbitrary omen lists.
- Emphasizes `理气` flow and contextual use (`用神`) over formula recitation.

### English synthesis
- Read as a precision philosophy of dynamic balancing, not as a superstition catalog.
- Interpretation must test whether chosen "use" is truly functional in the whole chart ecology.

---

## 14) 《穷通宝鉴》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `Medium-High`

### Core doctrine learned
- Seasonal climate and regulation (`调候`) are central.
- Ten stems across twelve months framework encodes practical seasonal adjustment logic.

### English synthesis
- This text is essential for thermal/moisture regulation logic in BaZi-style reading.
- It complements, rather than replaces, structural pattern analysis.

## 18) 朱熹《周易本义》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `High` for macro-positioning

### Core doctrine learned
- Reasserts Yi as rooted in divination (`卜筮`) while retaining principle discourse.
- Seeks disciplined balance of `象数` and `义理`.

### English synthesis
- Zhu Xi provides a bridge model: ritual/divinatory function plus philosophical coherence.
- Distinguishes textual layers to reduce category confusion.

## 19) 马王堆帛书《周易》

- Coverage: `Medium`
- Confidence: `Medium`

### Learned value
- Early manuscript witness with significant sequence and lexical differences from received text.
- Critical for textual criticism and historical reconstruction.

### English synthesis
- Claims of "single immutable Yi text" are not philologically defensible.
- Variant-aware reading increases historical accuracy.
